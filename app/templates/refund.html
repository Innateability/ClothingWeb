This policy is in accordance with your rights under the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Act (FCCPA) 2018 and the regulations of the Consumer Protection Council (CPC) of Nigeria.

1. Your Right to Return

Under the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Act (FCCPA) 2018, you have the right to return goods that are defective, not as described, or unfit for purpose. In addition to your statutory rights, we offer a voluntary return window of 7 days from the date of delivery for eligible items.

2. Eligibility for Returns

To be eligible for a return, your item must meet all of the following conditions:

  • Returned within 7 days of the delivery date
  • Unworn, unwashed, and unaltered
  • In original packaging with all tags still attached
  • Accompanied by proof of purchase (order number)

3. Non-Returnable Items

The following items cannot be returned unless they are defective or not as described:

  • Sale or discounted items marked as final sale
  • Underwear, lingerie, and swimwear (for hygiene reasons)
  • Accessories (belts, bags, jewellery)
  • Items that have been worn, washed, or had tags removed
  • Items returned after the 7-day window

4. How to Initiate a Return

To start a return:

  1. Contact us via the phone number on your order confirmation within 7 days of delivery
  2. Provide your order number and the reason for the return
  3. We will verify your eligibility and provide return instructions
  4. Package the item securely and send it to the address we provide

Note: Do not return items without first contacting us. Unauthorised returns may not be accepted.

5. Return Shipping Costs

Return shipping costs are the responsibility of the buyer, except in the following cases where we will cover return shipping:

  • You received a defective or damaged item
  • You received the wrong item
  • The item was significantly not as described

6. Refund Processing

Once we receive and inspect the returned item, we will notify you of the outcome within 48 hours. If your return is approved:

  • Refunds are processed within 3–5 business days
  • Refunds are made to your original payment method (bank transfer or Paystack reversal)
  • We do not refund original delivery charges unless the return is due to our error

If your return is rejected (e.g. item shows signs of wear), we will notify you and return the item to you at your expense.

7. Defective or Wrong Items

If you receive a defective, damaged, or incorrect item, you must contact us within 48 hours of delivery with:

  • Your order number
  • Clear photographs of the defect or wrong item
  • A brief description of the issue

We will offer you a replacement or full refund at no additional cost to you. This is in addition to your rights under the FCCPA 2018.

8. Exchanges

We offer size or colour exchanges subject to availability. Contact us with your order number and the item you would like to exchange for. If the requested item is available, we will process the exchange once the original item is returned and inspected. Exchange shipping costs are shared — return shipping is paid by the buyer, outbound shipping for the new item is covered by us.

9. Consumer Protection

If you feel your consumer rights have not been respected, you may contact the Consumer Protection Council (CPC) of Nigeria at cpc.gov.ng or call their national consumer helpline. You may also contact the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Commission (FCCPC) at fccpc.gov.ng.

10. Contact Us

For all return and refund queries, please contact us via the phone number shown on your order confirmation page. Our team is available Monday to Saturday, 9am to 6pm WAT.
