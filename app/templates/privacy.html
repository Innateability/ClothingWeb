This Privacy Policy explains how {{ admin.name if admin else '[Store Name]' }} collects, uses, and protects your personal data in compliance with the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR) 2019 and the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023.

1. Data Controller

{{ admin.name if admin else '[Store Name]' }} is the Data Controller responsible for your personal data. We are committed to protecting your privacy in accordance with Nigerian data protection law, specifically the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023 and the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR) 2019 issued by the National Information Technology Development Agency (NITDA).

2. What Personal Data We Collect

We collect the following personal data when you register or place an order:

  • Identity Data — your full name
  • Contact Data — email address, phone number
  • Delivery Data — street address, city, state
  • Transaction Data — order details, items purchased, payment amounts
  • Technical Data — IP address, browser type, session data (via cookies)

We do not collect sensitive personal data such as financial account details or government identification numbers.

3. Lawful Basis for Processing

We process your personal data on the following lawful bases under the NDPA 2023:

  • Contract — to fulfil your orders and provide delivery
  • Legitimate Interest — to improve our services and prevent fraud
  • Consent — where you have opted in to communications
  • Legal Obligation — where required by Nigerian law

4. How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data to:

  • Process and deliver your orders
  • Send order confirmations and delivery updates
  • Notify you when waitlisted items are back in stock
  • Handle returns and complaints
  • Improve your shopping experience
  • Comply with Nigerian legal and tax obligations

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Data Sharing

We share your data only with trusted service providers who assist us in operating our store, including:

  • Paystack — for secure payment processing (they operate under their own NDPR-compliant privacy policy)
  • Courier/Delivery Services — your name and address are shared with our delivery partners to fulfil orders

All third parties are required to protect your data and use it only for the purpose for which it was shared.

6. Payment Security

All online payments are processed securely through Paystack, a PCI-DSS compliant payment processor. We do not store your card details on our servers. All card transactions are encrypted using SSL technology.

7. Cookies

We use strictly necessary session cookies to keep you logged in and maintain your cart. These cookies expire when you close your browser or log out. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. By using our website you consent to the use of these essential cookies.

8. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide our services. Order records are retained for a minimum of 6 years in compliance with Nigerian tax and commercial law. You may request deletion of your account at any time, subject to legal retention obligations.

9. Your Rights Under the NDPA 2023

As a data subject under Nigerian law, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification — request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erasure — request deletion of your data where there is no legal basis to retain it
  • Restriction — request that we limit how we use your data
  • Portability — receive your data in a portable format
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interest
  • Withdraw Consent — withdraw consent at any time where processing is consent-based

To exercise any of these rights, contact us via the phone number on your order confirmation. We will respond within 30 days as required by the NDPA 2023.

10. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure. All passwords are stored in hashed form. Our website uses HTTPS encryption for all data in transit. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Complaints

If you believe your data protection rights have been violated, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) at ndpc.gov.ng or the National Information Technology Development Agency (NITDA) at nitda.gov.ng.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or Nigerian data protection law. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the updated policy on this page with a new effective date.

13. Contact

For any privacy-related queries or to exercise your data rights, contact us via the phone number shown on your order confirmation.
